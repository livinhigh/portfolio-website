GEMS-Singularity University Global Innovation Challenge 2017

Singularity University Global Summit | San Francisco, CA

This journey began as a grassroots initiative within our school and evolved into a global success story. Competing against a wide array of innovative student projects, our team of five developed a sustainable housing solution: portable, insulated cardboard homes designed to address the global refugee housing crisis. After securing the win at the intra-school level, we advanced to the GEMS-Singularity University Global Innovation Challenge, where we were named overall winners and received the People's Choice Award.

As a result of this achievement, our team was awarded a fully sponsored trip to San Francisco to attend the Singularity University Global Summit 2017. This prestigious event served as a platform for us to exhibit our project at the Innovation Hub, where we pitched our prototype to international investors, industry-leading tech innovators, and global thought leaders focused on exponential technologies.

Daksh Hackathon 2020

SASTRA University – Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

Representing Christ University (Bangalore Kengeri Campus), our team participated in the Daksh Hackathon 2020, a premier national-level competition hosted by SASTRA University.

The Project

We developed a high-impact application designed to solve real-world challenges through efficient code and user-centric design. During the development phase, we focused on scalability and seamless integration, ensuring the app met the rigorous technical standards expected at a national level.

The Evaluation

The competition was judged by a distinguished panel of experts, including industry professionals from PayPal and academic leaders from SASTRA University. Our project was evaluated on its technical architecture, problem-solving capabilities, and the viability of the final prototype.

The Result

Out of numerous teams representing top-tier institutions across India, we were awarded First Place. This victory highlighted our team's ability to work under pressure, translate complex ideas into functional software, and represent Christ University at the highest level of national competition.
